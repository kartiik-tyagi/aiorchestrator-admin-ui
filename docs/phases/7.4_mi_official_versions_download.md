# Phase 7.4 — MI Official tab: locked document, version picker, approved download; Approve fixes

**Wave:** MI minutes (MoM document)  
**Depends:** 7.3  
**Unlocks:** 7.5  
**Lock:** [PROGRAMME.md](./PROGRAMME.md); orchestrator doc 16 §4 OD-DOC-1/2, §5

---

## Operator outcome

In the Official tab I read the approved minutes, including decisions and action items, pick any approved version, see who approved it and when, and download it as a PDF labelled as approved minutes.

---

## Goal

Replace `OfficialPane` with `MinutesDocument` in locked mode plus a version picker and Download. Fix the two Approve gaps that stop the draft-to-official path.

---

## In scope

### Official tab

- Header: `Official vN` chip, approver label, `approved_at` (local time with zone), source draft version.  
- **Version picker** from `GET …/official/history` (rows already carry full packages; no extra fetch). Selected version in URL `&v=N`; default = current.  
- `MinutesDocument` with `mode="preview"`, no watermark, **decisions and actions shown** (missing today).  
- **Download ▾** with `officialVersion: N`.  
- Note when the draft moved on: "The draft has changes after Official vN (draft vM)." with a link to `?tab=mom`.  
- Empty state: "No approved version yet. Review the draft in the MoM tab, then approve."

### Approve tab fixes

- Preview response `soft_gates[]` → list them with a required checkbox "I have reviewed these". Confirm sends `acknowledge_soft_gates: true`. Today it never does, so any draft with soft gates fails with `409 soft_gates_unacknowledged`.  
- On success: toast, then navigate to `?tab=official&v=<new version>`.  
- Approve is disabled while the MoM tab is dirty: "Save or discard your edits first."

### Clean-up

- Delete `src/pages/mi/OfficialPane.jsx` (browser-side JSON download goes away; JSON comes from `DownloadMenu`).

---

## Out of scope

- Editing an Official (immutable by design)  
- Compare official vs draft (7.5)
